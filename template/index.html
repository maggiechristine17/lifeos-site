🧠 Brain dump

Drag the background to pan, pinch or scroll to zoom, drag a shape to move it, and tap one to select it: then tap other shapes to link or unlink them, or use the box at the top. Circles are projects, diamonds are areas, squares are resources, triangles are ideas, upside-down triangles are values, and rings are goals.
